# Theme Preferences

ainews supports 48 built-in themes (and optional custom themes) that you can switch between, including system mode that follows your OS dark/light preference.

## Finding the theme selector

The theme selector is in the top-right corner of every page. Click the palette icon to open it.

## Choosing a theme

- **System** — follows your operating system's dark/light mode setting
- **Built-in themes** — pre-installed themes curated by the project
- **Custom themes** — themes added by your administrator (if any)

Click a theme name to apply it immediately.

## System mode

When set to "System", ainews will:
- Use dark mode when your OS is in dark mode
- Use light mode when your OS is in light mode
- Switch automatically when your OS preference changes

## Your preference is saved

Your theme choice is saved in your browser and persists across visits. It is:
- Stored only on your device (not on the server)
- Device-specific (each browser/device has its own setting)
- Not linked to your account

## Resetting your preference

To reset to the default theme:
1. Open the theme selector
2. Select "System"

This will clear your custom selection and follow your OS preference.

## Custom themes

Your administrator may add custom themes. These appear alongside built-in themes in the selector. If a custom theme is removed by the administrator, your browser will automatically fall back to the system default.

## Theme choice does not affect content

Your theme selection only changes the visual appearance. It does not affect:
- Article content or summaries
- Your account or preferences
- Newsletter delivery
- Search history
